How do I see the full 3D effect?

Fold your iPhone into an L, with the half that has the rear camera standing up, and set it down. The upright half becomes a window: the rear camera shows the room behind, and the front camera follows your eyes so the hologram keeps its depth. Allow the camera when asked.

What does a hologram call need?

A FaceTime call with SharePlay, and HoloCall on both iPhones. Voices go through FaceTime, holograms through HoloCall.

I can't hear anything

Check the media volume and the silent switch. HoloCall raises quiet recordings to a normal level automatically.

How do I delete a hologram?

Touch and hold it in the library, then choose Delete.
