Privacy policy

HoloCall collects no personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Camera

Used to follow the position of your eyes so the hologram keeps its depth, and to film you when you choose to. Images are processed on your iPhone and never recorded unless you film yourself.

Microphone

Records your voice with the holograms you film of yourself.

Photos

Only the videos you pick are read, to turn them into holograms. Exported videos are saved to your library only when you ask.

Speech recognition

Captions are written on your iPhone. On older systems without on-device transcription, Apple's speech service may process the audio, under Apple's privacy policy.

Hologram calls

During a call, your hologram is sent to the people in the call through Apple's FaceTime and SharePlay, encrypted by Apple. Nothing passes through our servers: we have none.

Storage

Your holograms stay on your iPhone, in the app. Deleting the app deletes them.

Questions about privacy: https://github.com/jebibault/HoloCall/issues
